# Code of Conduct

We want Carbonized to be a place where people can work on climate and finance infrastructure without friction. This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Be respectful and specific. Critique code and ideas, not people. Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure about intent.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, or promoting tokens and unrelated products in issues and pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems by messaging [@CarbonizedApp](https://x.com/CarbonizedApp) and asking for a private channel. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and may block contributors who break these rules. Security vulnerabilities are handled separately through [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).
