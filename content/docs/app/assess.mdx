Route: `/app/assess`. Combines your onchain NFT credits with the emission inventory from the
[Emissions tracker](/docs/app/emissions) to plan how many tonnes to retire, reserve and sell. It
sends no transactions.

## ISO 14064 readiness

A self-assessment checklist, saved in `localStorage` per wallet (`carb-iso14064:v1:<wallet>`).
It is **not** a certification.

| Step | Completion rule |
| --- | --- |
| Inventory boundary defined | Checkbox |
| Base year set | Checkbox **and** a four-digit year between 1990 and the current year |
| Emission sources quantified | Detected automatically when the tracker has records for the selected year |
| Data quality and uncertainty documented | Checkbox |
| GHG inventory report prepared | Checkbox |
| Verified by a third party | Checkbox **and** auditor name, verification date and a document hash (`0x` + 64 hex characters, or an IPFS CID `Qm…` / `bafy…`) |

Ticked items with missing details show inline errors and do not count towards progress. The badge
turns to **Ready for verification** once all six steps are complete.

## Credits by registry standard

`getUserCredits(address)` reads every NFT you own and groups them by methodology (`VERRA_VCS`,
`GOLD_STANDARD`, `ICM_COMPLIANCE`). For each standard it shows the credit count, **eligible**
tonnes (not retired) and **retired** tonnes.

## Surplus allocation calculator

Let `E` be the year's emissions from the tracker and `C` the eligible (non-retired) NFT tonnes.

| Result | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Internal offset (retire to offset) | `min(C, E)` |
| Shortfall | `max(0, E − C)` |
| Surplus | `max(0, C − E)` |
| Reserve | `surplus × slider %` |
| Sellable surplus | `surplus − reserve` |
| Offset coverage | `min(C, E) / E` (shown as `—` when `E = 0`) |

The slider reserves a share of the surplus for future years or as a buffer. A donut chart shows
the split with the coverage in the centre, and a stacked bar compares emissions with credits.

Next steps from the calculator: **Retire credits** (`/app/carbon-wallet`), **Tokenize / wrap**
(`/app/tokenize`) and **Sell surplus** (`/app/sell-credits`).

<Callout type="info" title="Planning only">
  The calculator does not retire, wrap or sell anything. It uses your connected wallet's NFTs only,
  so credits already wrapped into pooled tokens are not counted as eligible.
</Callout>
