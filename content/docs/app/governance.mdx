Route: `/app/governance` (sidebar: **Governance**). It lets CARB holders follow and vote on proposals to the bounded protocol parameters. CARB is **not launched yet**; see [Governance](/docs/token/governance) for what can and cannot be governed.

## Configuration

Needs `NEXT_PUBLIC_CARB_TOKEN`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOVERNOR` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_TIMELOCK` (optional variables, not required by the missing-configuration banner). Without them the page shows **"CARB is not live yet"**. The guided form also uses the marketplace, staking and bond addresses (`NEXT_PUBLIC_CARBON_MARKETPLACE`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_CARB_STAKING`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_ISSUER_BOND`) as allowlisted targets.

## Proposals

Proposals are rebuilt from Governor events. Each card shows its state (pending, active, defeated, succeeded, queued, executed, canceled, expired), the for, against and abstain votes, and the quorum.

## Delegation

Voting power exists only after delegation. **Delegate** calls `CarbToken.delegate(...)` for yourself or another address. Staked and bonded CARB carry no votes. Votes count from the snapshot, so delegate before a proposal is created.

## Voting and execution

| Button | Contract call |
| --- | --- |
| **For**, **Against**, **Abstain** | `CarbGovernor.castVote(proposalId, support)` while the proposal is active |
| **Queue** | `CarbGovernor.queue(...)` once it has succeeded |
| **Execute** | `CarbGovernor.execute(...)` after the timelock delay |

Quorum is a fraction of total supply, including tokens that cannot vote.

## Propose parameter change

A guided form (a wallet holding at least the proposal threshold is needed) for **allowlisted targets only**:

- marketplace minimum price
- trading fee (basis points)
- KYC gate
- fee discount provider
- staking tier thresholds
- issuer bond minimum

The app encodes the calldata with viem and shows a **plain-language summary** of what the proposal will call before it sends `propose`. Targets outside the allowlist are blocked. Values outside the onchain bounds revert when executed, so the summary shows the bounds.

## States

| State | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| CARB addresses not set | "CARB is not live yet". |
| Wallet not connected | Proposals are visible; delegation, voting and proposing need a wallet. |
| No delegated votes | Voting buttons explain that delegation is needed. |
