Route: `/app/settings`. Language and the contract list work without a wallet; everything else needs
a connected wallet.

## Onchain verification

Shows whether your **company** has been KYB-reviewed in the `CarbonRegistry` (read with `getCompanyData`):
*KYB verified*, or *Awaiting verification* with the registration date. Traders are told that KYB
applies to companies. A KYB approval covers exactly the profile that the verifier reviewed
(`profileHashOf`); if you later change your name, legal ID, contact details or emissions, the
verified flag is reset and you need a new review.

If the connected wallet holds `VERIFIER_ROLE` on the registry, a **Verifier tools** box appears:
enter a company address, **Look up** its profile (name, legal ID, contact commitment hash, profile hash) and
**Mark as verified** or **Revoke verification** (`setCompanyVerified(company, verified, expectedProfileHash)`).
Approval is bound to the profile hash you looked at: if the company edits its profile in the
meantime the transaction is rejected (`Profile changed`) and you must review again. A legal ID can be
verified for one wallet only (`Legal ID already verified`). Other wallets never see this box, and
the contract rejects the call without the role.

## KYB checklist

Four documents: business registration (NIB or equivalent), tax ID (NPWP or equivalent), deed /
articles of incorporation, and directors / beneficial owners.

<Callout type="warn" title="Nothing is uploaded">
  Choosing a file computes its **SHA-256** in your browser with `crypto.subtle` (HTTPS or
  localhost). Only the file name, size, hash and time are stored, in `localStorage` under
  `carb-kyb:<wallet>`. The file itself is never stored or sent anywhere. The hash is stored
  locally; use **Download hashes (JSON)** and submit it to a verifier, who can ask you for the
  originals and compare. Files above 25 MB are rejected.
</Callout>

Each row shows *Hash stored locally* or *Not provided*, the full hash with a copy button, and
**Replace** / **Remove**. A progress bar counts hashed documents. The checklist is local
bookkeeping: completing it does not verify your company onchain.

## Wallet

Address (copy and explorer link, using the configured explorer), connected network (a warning appears when it differs from
`NEXT_PUBLIC_CHAIN_ID`), native balance and payment token balance (formatted with the token's `decimals()`).

## Organization profile

Read from the registry: company name, legal ID, **contact commitment (hash)**, Scope 1/2/3 totals, target and
registration dates, or the trader profile. Email and phone are not onchain, so they are not shown here.
Unregistered wallets get links to the company and trader registration pages.

## Preferences and contracts

Language (English / Chinese, shared with the top-bar toggle) and the list of configured
contract addresses with explorer links.
