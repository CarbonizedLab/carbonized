Route: `/app/token` (sidebar: **Token**). It shows the planned CARB utility token and lets you stake it for a fee tier. CARB is **not launched yet**; background is in the [CARB section](/docs/token).

</Callout>

## Configuration

Needs `NEXT_PUBLIC_CARB_TOKEN` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_CARB_STAKING`. These variables are optional and are **not** listed as required in the missing-configuration banner. If they are unset, the page shows a **"CARB is not live yet"** state and sends nothing. See [Environment variables](/docs/deployment/environment-variables).

## What it reads

| Item | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Total supply | `CarbToken` `totalSupply()` |
| Your CARB balance | `balanceOf(wallet)` |
| Your tier | `CarbStaking.tierOf(wallet)` |
| Staked amount, pending and matured stake, warm-up status, cooling amount and cooldown end | `positionOf(wallet)` and `effectiveStake(wallet)` |
| Tier table: thresholds and discounts | Read from the staking contract, not hard-coded. Discounts are shares **of the trading fee**. |

## Actions

| Button | Contract calls | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stake** | If the allowance is too low: `CarbToken.approve(staking, amount)`; then `CarbStaking.stake(amount)` | Approve-then-stake, no permit. New stake is pending until the warm-up has passed. |
| **Unstake** | `CarbStaking.unstake(amount)` | Lowers your tier immediately and starts (or restarts) the cooldown. |
| **Withdraw** | `CarbStaking.withdraw()` | Enabled after the cooldown; returns the cooled CARB. |

Durations are shown from the contract in seconds, converted to days and hours. Staked CARB carries **no votes**: delegate unstaked CARB on the [Governance page](/docs/app/governance) if you want to vote.

## States

| State | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| CARB addresses not set | "CARB is not live yet". |
| Wallet not connected | Supply and the tier table load; your position shows a connect prompt. |
| Warm-up | Pending stake is shown with the time until it counts toward the tier. |
| Cooldown | Cooling amount and the time until **Withdraw** is enabled. |
