**CarbGovernor inherits:** `Governor`, `GovernorSettings`, `GovernorCountingSimple`, `GovernorVotes`, `GovernorVotesQuorumFraction`, `GovernorTimelockControl` (OpenZeppelin 4.9.3).
**CarbTimelock inherits:** `TimelockController`. Sources: `contracts/token/CarbGovernor.sol`, `contracts/token/CarbTimelock.sol`.

Governance covers **protocol parameters only**. It has no control over credit issuance (the NFT's `ISSUER_ROLE` and `ISSUER_ADMIN_ROLE` are never given to it) and no control over user escrow (it holds no role that could touch it).

## How it works

- **Voting power:** [CARB](/docs/contracts/carb-token) with `ERC20Votes`. Tokens count only after `delegate(...)`. Tokens held by [`CarbStaking`](/docs/contracts/carb-staking) and [`IssuerBond`](/docs/contracts/issuer-bond) carry no votes.
- **Clock:** the token's clock is `block.timestamp`, so `votingDelay`, `votingPeriod`, `proposalSnapshot` and `proposalDeadline` are **seconds** (timestamps), not blocks.
- **Counting:** for / against / abstain (`GovernorCountingSimple`). Quorum is a percentage of the **total** supply at the proposal snapshot.
- **Execution:** a succeeded proposal is queued in `CarbTimelock` and executed after the timelock delay. The timelock, not the governor, is the account that holds `PARAMETER_ROLE` and `SLASHER_ROLE` on the protocol contracts, so governance power is exactly what those roles allow.

What the timelock can do (once the roles are granted at stage 1 or later, see [Roles & Security](/docs/contracts/roles-and-security#staged-decentralisation-of-the-carb-stack)):

| Contract | Via role | Functions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CarbonMarketplace` | `PARAMETER_ROLE` | `setTradingFee`, `setMinPricePerTonne`, `setKYCGate`, `setFeeDiscountProvider` |
| `CarbStaking` | `PARAMETER_ROLE` | `setTierThresholds`, `setTierDiscounts` |
| `IssuerBond` | `PARAMETER_ROLE` | `setMinBond`, `setBondRequired`, `setSlashTreasury` |
| `IssuerBond` | `SLASHER_ROLE` | `proposeSlash`, `cancelSlash`, `executeSlash` |
| Governor / timelock | self | bounded settings, `setTargetAllowed`, `updateDelay` |

Every setter keeps its own hard bounds, so a passed proposal cannot go outside them (for example trading fee at most 1 %).

## CarbGovernor

### Constructor

```solidity
constructor(
    IVotes token_,
    TimelockController timelock_,
    uint256 votingDelay_,
    uint256 votingPeriod_,
    uint256 proposalThreshold_,
    uint256 quorumPercent_,
    address[] memory initialTargets
)
```

The governor is named `CarbGovernor`. The constructor allowlists itself, the timelock and every address in `initialTargets` (the deployment helper passes marketplace, staking and bond). The bounds below are checked at construction and on every update, so the constructor can revert with their messages; `allowedTargets` rejects the zero address (`Invalid target`).

### Bounds (constants)

| Name | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `MIN_VOTING_DELAY` / `MAX_VOTING_DELAY` | 1 hour / 7 days |
| `MIN_VOTING_PERIOD` / `MAX_VOTING_PERIOD` | 1 day / 14 days |
| `MAX_PROPOSAL_THRESHOLD` | 50,000,000 CARB |
| `MIN_QUORUM_PERCENT` / `MAX_QUORUM_PERCENT` | 1 / 20 |

<Callout title="Default in deployment helper, owner may change before launch">
  `scripts/lib/governance-setup.js` uses a voting delay of 1 day, a voting period of 5 days, a proposal threshold of 1,000,000 CARB, a quorum of 4 % and a timelock delay of 2 days. These are owner parameters, bounded onchain, and may change before launch. Published values will be listed at launch (`contracts/contracts/docs/TOKEN.md`, parameters table).
</Callout>

### Target allowlist

`allowedTargets(address)` is a public mapping. `propose` reverts unless **every** target is allowlisted and **every** `values[i]` is zero, so a proposal cannot call other contracts or carry ETH. The allowlist is a second line of defence: even if governance voted to allowlist the NFT, executing an issuance grant would fail because the timelock holds no NFT role (covered by a test).

### Functions

| Function | Access | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `propose(targets, values, calldatas, description)` | proposer with at least `proposalThreshold()` votes | Enforces allowlist and zero value, then the OpenZeppelin proposal logic |
| `castVote`, `castVoteWithReason`, `castVoteWithReasonAndParams`, `castVoteBySig` | voters | support `0` against, `1` for, `2` abstain |
| `queue(targets, values, calldatas, descriptionHash)` | anyone | Schedules a succeeded proposal in the timelock |
| `execute(targets, values, calldatas, descriptionHash)` | anyone | Executes a queued proposal through the timelock once the delay has passed |
| `cancel(targets, values, calldatas, descriptionHash)` | proposer, while the proposal is pending | Cancels the proposal |
| `state(proposalId)` | view | `Pending`, `Active`, `Canceled`, `Defeated`, `Succeeded`, `Queued`, `Expired`, `Executed` |
| `proposalSnapshot`, `proposalDeadline`, `proposalVotes`, `hasVoted`, `getVotes`, `quorum(timepoint)`, `votingDelay`, `votingPeriod`, `proposalThreshold`, `quorumNumerator`, `quorumDenominator`, `timelock`, `token` | view | standard OpenZeppelin getters |
| `setTargetAllowed(target, allowed)` | `onlyGovernance` (a passed proposal) | Adds or removes an allowlisted target; `Invalid target` for the zero address |
| `setVotingDelay`, `setVotingPeriod`, `setProposalThreshold`, `updateQuorumNumerator`, `updateTimelock` | `onlyGovernance` | Bounded settings; `updateTimelock` is the standard `GovernorTimelockControl` function |

Hash a proposal with `hashProposal(targets, values, calldatas, keccak256(bytes(description)))`.

### Errors (revert strings)

| Message | Where |
| --- | --- |
| `Target not allowed` | `propose` with a non-allowlisted target |
| `ETH value not allowed` | `propose` with a non-zero value |
| `Invalid target` | `setTargetAllowed`, constructor allowlist |
| `Voting delay out of bounds` | delay outside 1 hour to 7 days |
| `Voting period out of bounds` | period outside 1 day to 14 days |
| `Threshold too high` | proposal threshold above 50,000,000 CARB |
| `Quorum out of bounds` | quorum outside 1 % to 20 % |

Everything else is standard OpenZeppelin `Governor:` / `TimelockController:` reverts (for example `Governor: proposer votes below proposal threshold`, `Governor: proposal not successful`).

## CarbTimelock

An OpenZeppelin `TimelockController` whose minimum delay is **bounded**, so governance can never collapse the public exit window to zero.

```solidity
constructor(uint256 minDelay, address[] memory proposers, address[] memory executors, address admin)
```

| Item | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| `MIN_DELAY_FLOOR` / `MAX_DELAY_CAP` | 1 day / 30 days |
| Constructor | reverts `Delay out of bounds` if `minDelay` is outside the range |
| `getMinDelay()` | overridden; returns the bounded delay tracked by this contract. `schedule` and `scheduleBatch` use it. |
| `updateDelay(newDelay)` | overridden; only the timelock itself (`TimelockController: caller must be timelock`), reverts `Delay out of bounds`; emits `MinDelayChange` |
| `schedule`, `scheduleBatch`, `execute`, `executeBatch`, `cancel` | standard `TimelockController` (roles `PROPOSER_ROLE`, `EXECUTOR_ROLE`, `CANCELLER_ROLE`) |
| Events | standard (`CallScheduled`, `CallExecuted`, `Cancelled`, `MinDelayChange`, ...) |

The timelock is intended to be **self-administered**: pass `admin = address(0)`, or renounce `TIMELOCK_ADMIN_ROLE` after wiring. The deployment helper (`deployGovernance`) grants the governor `PROPOSER_ROLE` and `CANCELLER_ROLE`, grants `EXECUTOR_ROLE` to `address(0)` (anyone may execute a queued operation) unless configured otherwise, and renounces the deployer's temporary `TIMELOCK_ADMIN_ROLE`.

It holds no credit-issuance, escrow or admin role. `assertGovernanceBoundaries` in the same helper fails if the timelock ever holds a forbidden role.

## Caveats

- **Governance capture.** One token, one vote. If the owner, treasury or a few holders control most delegated supply, they control the outcome. Bounds and the least-privilege timelock limit what a captured vote can do (fee at most 1 %, discount at most 50 % of the fee, no issuance, no escrow). They do not prevent slashing bonded issuers or changing the KYC-gate and minimum-price parameters.
- **Quorum vs. non-voting supply.** Quorum is a fraction of total supply, including treasury, staked and bonded tokens that cannot vote. Calibrate `quorumPercent` against the real distribution of **delegated** tokens or governance can stall.
- **Delegation is required.** Undelegated tokens have no votes.
- **Timestamps.** The sequencer sets `block.timestamp` on an L2 within bounded drift; durations are days, so drift is immaterial, but a sequencer outage delays proposal progress.
- **Emergency powers stay off-governance.** `PAUSER_ROLE` intentionally stays with a fast multisig at every stage.
- Not upgradeable.
