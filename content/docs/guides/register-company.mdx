---
title: Register a company
description: Register your company onchain. Email and phone stay in your browser; only a hash goes onchain.
---

Company registration writes your company profile and reported emissions to the onchain **CarbonRegistry**. It does **not** mint or give you any credits: there is no starter pack. You obtain credits by [buying them](/docs/guides/buy-credits) or from an authorized issuer.

<Callout type="warn" title="What becomes public">
  Company name, legal entity ID and emission figures are public and permanent on the blockchain. Email and phone are **not** published: the app computes `contactHash = keccak256(abi.encode(email, phone, salt))` with a random salt and stores only the hash onchain. The email, phone and salt are kept in this browser's local storage.
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

- Wallet connected on the target network with a little gas ETH.
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_CARBON_REGISTRY` configured.
- A wallet with no existing registry profile: each wallet is **either one company or one trader**, once.
- No backend is required. Leave `NEXT_PUBLIC_BACKEND_URL` empty unless you run your own: when it is set, the registration details, including email and phone, are sent to that server.

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the form

Go to `/app/company/register` and connect your wallet.

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in the details

Required: company name, legal entity ID, email (contains `@`), phone. Optional: Scope 1, 2 and 3 annual emissions in tonnes CO₂e. The form shows the total. The name, legal ID and emissions are stored onchain and drive the company dashboard's coverage figures.

</Step>
<Step>

### Submit

Click **Register Company**. The app generates the salt, computes the contact hash and calls `CarbonRegistry.registerCompany(name, legalId, scope1, scope2, scope3, contactHash)`; one wallet signature, and the app waits for the receipt. Email, phone and salt are then saved in your browser.

</Step>
<Step>

### Verify

You are on the company dashboard, which shows your profile, emissions, your contact commitment (hash) and a **KYB Pending** badge until a verifier reviews your profile.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Details worth knowing

- **KYB review.** A `VERIFIER_ROLE` account approves the profile hash it reviewed (`profileHashOf`). It is a review of what you submitted, not a guarantee about your company.
- **Editing resets KYB.** Re-opening the page after registering shows **Save Changes Onchain**. Changing your name, legal entity ID, contact details or any Scope 1/2/3 figure calls `updateCompanyProfile` / `updateEmissions` and resets the KYB flag; a verifier must review again. Leave email and phone empty to keep the current contact hash.
- **One verified wallet per legal ID.** A legal ID can be verified for one wallet at a time.
- **Local contact data.** If you clear this browser's storage you lose your email, phone and salt; the onchain hash stays.

## If it fails

| Symptom | Cause |
| --- | --- |
| "Registry not configured" | `NEXT_PUBLIC_CARBON_REGISTRY` is missing. Set it and restart the dev server. |
| `Already registered` | This wallet already has a company or trader profile. Use another wallet. |
| `Pausable: paused` | The registry is paused by its operators. |
| Revert on name, legal ID or emissions | A field is empty or over the limit (name 100, legal ID 64 characters; each emission at most 1 trillion tonnes). |
