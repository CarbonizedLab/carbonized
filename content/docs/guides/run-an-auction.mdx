---
title: Run an auction
description: Create an auction as a seller, bid as a buyer, and settle it.
---

An auction sells a block of CCT to the highest **total** payment token bid. The seller's CCT and the top bid's payment token are both held by `CarbonMarketplace` until settlement.

## As a seller

<Steps>
<Step>

### Have CCT

Wrap NFT credits first (see [Sell credits](/docs/guides/sell-credits)). You need a CCT balance covering the auction amount.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create the auction

On `/app/sell-credits` choose **Auction**. Enter the amount (tonnes), **Starting Bid** (a *total* in payment token for the whole lot) and an **End Date** (browser local time) that is **between 1 hour and 30 days from now**. Click **Create Listing**. After an optional CCT `approve`, the app calls `createAuction(amount, startPrice, endTime)` with `endTime` as an absolute Unix timestamp, which locks the CCT.

</Step>
<Step>

### Watch it

The auction appears at `/app/auctions` with a **Seller** badge and **Seller Controls**: highest bid, bidder, and a bid-history link.

</Step>
<Step>

### Settle

- **No bids:** click **Cancel Auction** any time (`cancelAuction`), or wait for the end and call finalize; the CCT returns to you.
- **With bids:** you cannot cancel. After the end time click **Accept & Finalize**, which calls `finalizeAuction`. You receive the highest bid minus the 0.25 % default fee (the rate that applied when the auction was created); the winner receives the CCT. If the payment token refuses to pay you, the proceeds become claimable with **Claim**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## As a bidder

<Steps>
<Step>

### Have payment token and gas

See [Buy credits](/docs/guides/buy-credits#get-test-usdc) for test payment token (testnet only); on mainnet you need real payment token.

</Step>
<Step>

### Place a bid

Open `/app/auctions`, click **Place Bid**, and enter your **total bid in payment token**. It must be at least the starting price and strictly higher than the current bid. The app approves payment token if needed (`approve(marketplace, bid)`), then calls `placeBid(auctionId, bidAmount)`. The payment token is escrowed.

</Step>
<Step>

### If you are outbid

Your payment token is refunded automatically in the same transaction as the higher bid (if the token refuses the transfer, the refund appears as a **Claim** banner on the marketplace). The card shows **Outbid** and a **Place Higher Bid** button.

</Step>
<Step>

### Claim

After the timer ends, the winner sees **Finalize & Claim CCT** and calls `finalizeAuction`. The CCT is transferred to the winning wallet.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Anyone can finalize">
  The contract lets any address call `finalizeAuction` once `endTime` has passed. The UI exposes the
  button to the seller and the highest bidder. An auction stays in the list, marked as ended, until
  it is finalized.
</Callout>

## If it fails

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Duration too short`, `Duration too long` | End date is not between 1 hour and 30 days from now. |
| `Bid below start price`, `Bid too low` | Bid under the start price or not above the highest bid. |
| `Auction ended` | Bid sent after `endTime`. |
| `Auction not ended` | Finalize attempted early. |
| `Auction has bids` | Cancel attempted after a bid. |

Reference: [Auctions page](/docs/app/auctions)
