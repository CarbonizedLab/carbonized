CARB is a **utility token** for the Carbonized carbon-credit protocol on Robinhood Chain. It has one fixed supply, minted once at deployment; the exact parameters are published at launch. It has three jobs and nothing else.

## What CARB does

| Utility | Contracts | Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fee tiers** | `CarbStaking`, `CarbonMarketplace` | Stake CARB to hold a tier (0 to 3). A higher tier lowers the marketplace **trading fee** you pay as a seller, by at most 50 percent **of the fee**. Nothing is paid out to stakers. See [Staking and tiers](/docs/token/staking-and-tiers). |
| **Issuer bond** | `IssuerBond`, `CarbonCreditNFT` | An alternative way to be granted the issuer role: lock a slashable CARB bond. Only when the owner opens that path. See [Issuer bond](/docs/token/issuer-bond). |
| **Parameter governance** | `CarbGovernor`, `CarbTimelock` | CARB holders vote on a short list of bounded protocol parameters. See [Governance](/docs/token/governance). |

The discount applies to the fee, never to the price of a credit. It applies to the CCT **seller** on order-book and auction sales, not to AMM swaps.

## What CARB does not do

No revenue share, rewards, APY, buy-back or mint function; no control over credit issuance or user escrow; not a claim on credits or on any treasury. The full list is on [What CARB does not do](/docs/token/what-carb-does-not-do).

## Technical facts

- ERC-20 with ERC-2612 permit, ERC-5805 votes and burn when deployed as the Carbonized token. Supply can only decrease (holders burning, and slashing, which burns by default).
- The voting clock is `block.timestamp`. Robinhood Chain is an Arbitrum Orbit L2 where block numbers are not a wall-clock proxy, so every duration is in **seconds**.
- Voting power counts only after `delegate(...)` (self-delegation is enough). **Staked and bonded CARB carry no votes.**
- The Carbonized token contract has no owner, no pause, no blacklist, no transfer tax and no upgradeability.
- If CARB is launched later through a third-party launchpad, it is an external ERC-20 that Carbonized does not control, and voting goes through a wrapper. See [Using an existing token (launchpad)](/docs/token/external-token).
